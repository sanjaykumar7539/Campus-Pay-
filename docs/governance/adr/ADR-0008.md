# ADR-0008: Tenant Context, Kiosk Device Foundation and Scoped Identity

**ADR ID:** ADR-0008  
**Title:** Tenant derivation, per-institution Campus IDs and minimal kiosk Device  
**Status:** Proposed (pending approval)  
**Date:** 21 September 2026  
**Decision Makers:** CampusPay Engineering Team

---

# Context

The MVP selected `Institution.first()` at login and registration, looked up Campus IDs globally, listed all tenants' data to any admin, and used a globally unique payment reference. ADR-0001 requires that tenant isolation is never bypassed.

# Decision

1. Login takes `institution_code` + username; the tenant is carried in the token and revalidated per request. Services take an explicit `institution_id`; a tenant-bound session also appends `institution_id` to every ORM SELECT of tenant-scoped entities (backstop).
2. Identity boundary is (Institution, Campus ID): `campus_users(institution_id, unique_identifier)`, `credentials(institution_id, username)`, `payments(institution_id, reference)`, `orders(institution_id, client_request_key)` are unique **per institution**.
3. Device: a kiosk is a tenant-scoped, activatable `devices` row assigned to a merchant; many kiosks per merchant; Merchant ≠ Kiosk. Device depends on Institution only (assignment is by id). The kiosk **authentication mechanism is OPEN**: no device credential scheme is invented; kiosks are operated under an authenticated staff session and may reference a registered device (`X-Device-Id`).
4. Merchant staff are scoped to their assigned merchant; ADMIN is not unrestricted financial authority (no wallet/ledger mutation route exists).

# Consequences

Cross-tenant access yields 404; same Campus ID or username can exist in two institutions. Kiosk hardware authentication requires a later ADR.
