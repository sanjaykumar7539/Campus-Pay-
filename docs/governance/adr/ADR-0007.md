# ADR-0007: Campus Unit Representation

**ADR ID:** ADR-0007  
**Title:** ₹1 = 1 Campus Unit; integer Campus Units are the native financial unit  
**Status:** Proposed (pending approval)  
**Date:** 21 September 2026  
**Decision Makers:** CampusPay Engineering Team

---

# Context

₹1 = 1 Campus Unit is an approved financial concept. The MVP stored money as integer paise (`*_minor`), so a Campus Unit could be mistaken for 100 stored units.

# Decision

1. The Campus Unit (CU) is the native unit of ledger, wallet, order, payment and receipt amounts; new columns are `*_cu` BIGINT holding **whole** CUs. No floating point.
2. Fractional CUs are not modelled: a sub-unit amount is rejected, never rounded. Any future precision requirement needs a new ADR.
3. `shared/campus_units.py` is the only place that converts legacy paise. Migration `0002` converts exactly (÷100) and **refuses** non-whole amounts or wallet/history mismatches; `*_minor` columns are dropped, not renamed in bulk.
4. The deprecated manual-payment endpoint accepts paise only to convert at its boundary.

# Consequences

Unambiguous semantics; whole-rupee pricing only in P1.
